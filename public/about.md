# About ScoutLens

ScoutLens is an independent football scouting project built by Dilip Badal, with React, TypeScript and FastAPI. Choose a club, position and role across Europe's five major leagues, rank candidates and compare players through interactive graphs and recorded numbers.

I’m building ScoutLens to turn football data into a clearer recruitment conversation: who fits the role, how they compare, and what the numbers can actually tell us.

## Scope

Historical data for 2024/25 and 2025/26. Scores support research and do not replace watching matches or professional judgment. ScoutLens is not affiliated with Football Manager or its credited data providers.
